## How to make an ad campaign with Roblox Ads Manager: Choosing audiences ([1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5HcH-9E7USc&t=64s))

Once you've picked your goal, now it's time to pick your audience. Now, you can leave this open and let Roblox find users for you, or you can select it manually. Selecting new users will show your ad to people who have never played your game or haven't played in the last 180 days. Selecting recent users will only show your ad to people who have played in the last 30 days. And selecting lapsed users will reach players who played between 30 and 180 days ago and haven't come back. If your experience is new, we recommend not selecting any audiences and letting Roblox find them for you. If your experience has been around for a while, we recommend selecting new users to grow your base. And if your experience just published an update, we recommend lapsed users to bring people back.
