## What's the Creator Store?: Creator Store Payouts ([1:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NAwQr9sTy74&t=111s))

First, if you haven't already, navigate to the home page and create a Roblox account. And make sure to complete ID verification to meet Roblox's public asset sharing requirements. Once you've done that, you'll need to create a third-party Stripe account if you do not have one already. Keep in mind, you can only link one Stripe account to one store seller account. And due to tax restrictions, your Stripe account will be locked to the region you select while signing up. To link your Stripe account on the Creator Store, start by clicking on your profile image on the top right, then clicking settings, navigating to the eligibility tab, then clicking on priced assets, and verify you meet all the prerequisites listed on the top of the page. The current prerequisites include being ID verified, being 18+ or 13+ with parental permission, not having any recent moderation history, and residing in a supporting country. If you meet all requirements, you can continue to fill out the form and finish linking your account by clicking the continue button on the bottom of the page. Now that your account's set up, when do you get paid out? When a customer buys your asset, the money from the sale will enter a 30-day escrow period. At the end of that holding period, the money will get added to your Stripe account balance and will get deposited in linked bank account in the next payout cycle. If your seller account is denominated in a non-USD currency, the currency conversion will happen as each sales proceeds are transferred into your Stripe balance.
