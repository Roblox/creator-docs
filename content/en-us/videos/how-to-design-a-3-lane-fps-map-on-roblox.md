## How to design a 3-lane FPS map on Roblox: What is 3-lane design? ([0:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fGaiAvh7Q-4&t=13s))

Let's dive in. Most popular games use a three-lane design system to lay out their maps, some more literally than others. At its core, a map made this way has three main paths connecting both spawn points, as well as secondary paths that connect them in more complex ways. This allows players to easily remember the map layouts, understand where they're meant to go, and provide fast-paced, competitive gameplay.
